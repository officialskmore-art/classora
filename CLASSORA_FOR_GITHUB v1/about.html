FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Clarifications on accounts, Classora IDs, and platform usage.

What is the difference between my Email and my Classora ID? ▾
Your email address and password are your private authentication credentials used only to log into Classora. Your Classora ID (e.g. TC5839201746 or ST7416382059) is your public academic badge. You share it with students or teachers so they can discover and connect with you without sharing your login details.
Can students join multiple classrooms across different teachers? ▾
Yes! A student maintains one permanent Classora ID and can belong to multiple classrooms (e.g., Physics with Ms. Sharma, Chemistry with Mr. Patel). Each classroom is isolated with its own syllabus, assignments, and grades.
How does student onboarding work? ▾
Students can pre-register independently on Classora to receive their ID. Then they either send a join request to a classroom code or the teacher invites them directly using their Student ID. Teachers approve requests with a single click.
Can I change my registered email address later? ▾
Yes. Classora permits email changes inside your Profile settings, protected by mandatory dual-email verification to guarantee account security.
Are exam results published automatically or manually? ▾
Teachers input marks in draft mode. When finalized, the teacher clicks "Publish Results," which instantly updates the student portals and triggers automated notifications.
