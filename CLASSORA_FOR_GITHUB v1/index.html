✨ Features

Everything you need,
in one place

From classroom creation to progress tracking — Classora gives teachers and students a single organised workspace.

🏫

Classroom Management

Create and manage multiple classrooms with ease. Invite students by Classora ID or approve join requests in one click.

📅

Schedule & Calendar

Organise class timetables and academic events. Students instantly see today's and upcoming schedules on their dashboard.

📝

Exams & Homework

Schedule examinations and assign homework. Track submission and review upcoming deadlines in a clean view.

📊

Results & Progress

Enter exam results, view averages and totals, and track student progress based on real academic data — not arbitrary scores.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Stay updated with in-app notifications for exam schedules, result publications, join requests, and classroom updates.

🪪

Classora ID System

Every user receives a permanent, unique Classora ID (TC... for teachers, ST... for students) — a stable identity that never changes.

🚀 How It Works

Up and running in minutes

Simple onboarding for both teachers and students with no technical expertise required.

1

Create Your Account

Register as a teacher or student with your email and password, or sign in with Google. Email verification is required.

2

Set Up Your Space

Teachers automatically get an independent workspace. Create classrooms and invite students using their Classora ID.

3

Manage & Track

Schedule classes, assign homework, run exams, publish results, and track student progress — all from your dashboard.

🪪 Classora ID

Your permanent
academic identity

Every user receives a unique, permanent Classora ID the moment they register. It's your identity inside Classora — not your login credential, just who you are.

🔒
Identity, not authentication
Classora ID identifies you — it never replaces your password
♾️
Permanent and stable
Your ID stays the same even if you change teacher or classroom
